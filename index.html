A Letter To My Future Wife

Gargi,

If someone had told me back when I was avoiding marriage and dodging profiles on Shaadi.com that a single picture of a girl in a red saree would completely flip my life upside down, I would have laughed at them. Yet here I am, the guy who used to tease everyone else for texting their partners, completely, hopelessly head over heels for you.

It’s funny how our story started. From me nervously googling topics to talk about and calling you “ma’am” on April 2nd, to sneaking your photos off Mummy’s phone just to see how we’d look standing next to each other, everything about us has felt like pure serendipity. Who would have thought that two people with opposite opinions on ducks—where you think they’re fluffy and cute and I still firmly believe they’re evil—could be such an exact mirror of each other?

You have no idea how effortlessly you’ve become my entire world. I’ve spent so much of my life trying to be strong on my own, fixing every problem by myself, and being the shield for my family. Then you walked in with your infectious energy, your late-night overthinking, your clumsy self bumping into trees, and that brilliant, ambitious mind, and taught me that I don’t have to do it all alone anymore. You asked me once what I wanted in a partner, and I told you I didn’t want an arranged marriage; I wanted to fall madly in love with my best friend. You gave me that without me even realizing when it happened.

I love everything about who you are, Kiddo. I love that you’re my safe space and that you let me be yours when the world gets too loud or when you just need extra snuggles and dark chocolate. I love how fiercely you care for the people around you, how effortlessly you’ve already won over Mummy, Papa, and Anmol, and how even Shivji seems to have aligned the universe to bring you to me.

You once told me that before our engagement, you wanted a proper, quiet proposal, nothing extravagant, just the two of us, me asking you officially to be mine. So here I am, keeping my promise, laying my heart completely bare in front of you.

I want to be the man who walks beside you, literally, never opposite you, through every season of this life. I want to hold your tiny hands when we’re crossing busy roads, carry your Crocs when your heels start hurting, kiss your forehead when you’re stressed, and watch you shine the day you finally add “Dr.” before your name. I want our long drives with Duggu, our quiet coffee dates, our annual walks to Vaishno Devi, and a lifetime of laughing together until we’re old and grey.

I know I’m not always the most patient man, and I’ll probably keep forgetting dates while you keep track of them on your Excel sheets, but I promise you this: I will never stop choosing you, protecting you, and loving you louder every single day. Tumhare bina ab mera koi wajood nahi lagta, aur main rehna bhi nahi chahta.

No return or replacement policies allowed, Miss Gargi Shukla, you’re stuck with me. Will you marry me, officially become Mrs. Gargi Anurag Tripathi, and let me spend the rest of my life making you the happiest woman in the world?

Forever yours,
Anurag ♥
